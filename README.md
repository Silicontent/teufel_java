# Teufel Sechs: The Ultimatum
The sixth and final version of the "Pain Pack," a complete overhaul of Minecraft that makes it as difficult as possible. No, you don't have to play the other versions of the mod first.

Originally a port of Teufel Fünf, the fifth pack in the [Teufel series](https://github.com/Silicontent/teufel_collection) of Minecraft Bedrock addons. As of right now, this mod is IN DEVELOPMENT. Everything you may see if you decide to download and build this is subject to change.

### Warning! Mod Incompatibility & Dependencies
This mod is inherently NOT COMPATIBLE with other mods, especially mods containing new/modified content. I do not recommend including this mod in any kind of modpack, as things may break! As you might expect, this mod requires the **Fabric API** in order to function.
